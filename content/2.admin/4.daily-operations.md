Once buses are running, the admin side is mostly about watching and responding.

## Live monitoring

**Live Monitoring** (`/admin/monitoring`) shows every active trip on one map: where each bus is, how many people are on board, and anything that needs attention.

![Live monitoring](/images/admin/13-live-map.webp)

The page updates by itself. The server pushes changes over a live connection (Reverb) on the `admin.operations` channel. Bus positions come from the driver app's GPS, and on-board counts come from kiosk taps.

The overview panel flags:

- **SOS**: a driver pressed the emergency button.
- **Reports**: a driver reported a non-emergency problem.
- **Stale GPS**: a bus hasn't sent its position for a while (usually the phone lost signal).

Staff can **acknowledge** an SOS or report, **message** the driver straight from it, and **correct** a stop time the driver marked wrongly.

Every 5 minutes the server also checks for problems and raises a notification (bell icon) for things like a bus that's nearly full, a kiosk that's been offline too long, or a burst of duplicate taps (`notifications:check-operational-alerts`).

Code: `LiveMonitoringController`, `TripSosController`, `IncidentReportController`, and the page in `resources/js/pages/Admin/Monitoring/`.

## Messaging drivers

**Messages** is a chat between dispatch and drivers. Drivers read and reply in the driver app.

- Conversations are only ever between staff and a driver, never driver to driver.
- **Broadcasts** send one message to many drivers at once (for example everyone on a route).
- To keep drivers safe, the driver app won't let them type while the bus is moving.
- Kiosk enrollment codes can be sent as a **secure message** that the driver reveals once.

Code: `app/Actions/Messaging/`, plus the `driver.{id}` and `conversation.{id}` live channels.

## Announcements

**Announcements** are notices for passengers or staff, such as a road closure or a holiday schedule. Each one has a title and message (in English and Filipino), an audience, an optional route, and a start and end time.

An announcement shows nowhere until it's **published**. A job runs every minute to show scheduled ones and hide expired ones (`announcements:sweep`). Always set an end time, or it stays up.

## Reports

**Reports** (`/admin/reports`) shows ridership by date, route, bus and passenger group (regular, student, senior, PWD), and can export to PDF or Excel. It can also produce an official letterhead report (Word or PDF), which is saved to the report archive.

Things to know:

- Reports read from summary tables built **overnight** at 01:00 (`AggregateRidershipSummaries`). Today's numbers show up tomorrow. To rebuild a date range, run `php artisan reports:aggregate --from=... --to=...`.
- Ridership counts **boardings only**, not alightings.
- A trip that was **never ended** is not counted. That's why drivers are told to always end their trip.

**Trip Reports** (`/admin/trips`) lists every trip with its stops, times and taps. Use it to answer questions about one particular trip.

## Audit logs

**Audit Logs** records who did what: schedule changes, kiosk codes issued, roles given, secure codes revealed, and so on. When something changes and nobody knows why, look here first.
